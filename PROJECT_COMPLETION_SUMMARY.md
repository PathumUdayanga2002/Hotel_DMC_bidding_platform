## 📞 Support & Maintenance

### Common Questions

**Q: Why staggered delays?**
A: Creates visual rhythm, guides user attention, feels intentional

**Q: Why parallax?**
A: Adds depth, improves engagement, modern aesthetic

**Q: Why dark overlays?**
A: Ensures text readability, maintains visual hierarchy

**Q: Mobile performance?**
A: Parallax disabled on mobile, animations optimized for performance

**Q: How to customize?**
A: All delays are inline (`style={{animationDelay}}`), easy to adjust
